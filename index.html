Nearby Attractions

RK Palace is conveniently located near the top tourist attractions in Puri. Guests can reach the beautiful Sea Beach within 1 minute walking distance and enjoy relaxing beach views. Chandi Bari is also nearby, while the famous Light House is just 5 minutes away. The sacred Jagannath Temple and Lokanatha Temple are only 15 minutes from the hotel. Mata Matha / Goura Vihar Ashram is around 10 minutes away. RK Palace is an ideal hotel near Puri Beach and Jagannath Temple for family stays, holidays, and spiritual trips.
