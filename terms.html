Guest Policies

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to RK Palace. By booking and staying at our hotel, guests agree to follow all hotel rules, policies, and guidelines. Our goal is to provide a safe, comfortable, and peaceful environment for every guest. Please read all terms carefully before confirming your reservation or using any hotel services during your stay.

2. Check-In & Check-Out

Guests must provide a valid government-issued ID proof during check-in as per hotel policy and local regulations. Standard check-in and check-out timings should be followed. Early check-in or late check-out requests are subject to room availability and management approval. Additional charges may apply for extended stay hours depending on hotel policies and seasonal availability.

3. Use of Property

Guests are requested to use hotel rooms, furniture, and amenities responsibly during their stay. Any damage caused to hotel property, equipment, or room items due to negligence or misconduct will be charged to the guest accordingly. Respectful behavior toward hotel staff, management, and other guests is expected at all times inside the hotel premises.

4. Room Policies

Smoking, illegal activities, loud noise, parties, or any disturbance affecting other guests are strictly prohibited inside the hotel premises. Guests must maintain cleanliness and proper conduct throughout their stay. Hotel management reserves the right to refuse service, cancel bookings, or ask guests to leave if hotel policies or safety guidelines are violated.

5. Payments & Bookings

All room bookings are subject to availability and confirmation after successful payment. Guests may be required to pay advance booking amounts during weekends, holidays, or peak travel seasons. Cancellation, refund, and modification requests will be processed according to hotel booking policies. Prices and room tariffs may change without prior notice depending on seasonal demand.

6. Safety & Security

Guests are responsible for keeping their valuables, money, and personal belongings safe during their stay. RK Palace management will not be responsible for any loss, theft, or damage of personal items inside rooms or hotel premises. For guest safety, CCTV monitoring and security measures may be active in selected common areas.

7. Policy Updates

RK Palace reserves the right to update, modify, or change hotel policies, room tariffs, services, and terms & conditions at any time without prior notice. Guests are advised to review the latest policies before booking or staying at the hotel. Continued use of our services will be considered acceptance of the updated terms and conditions provided by hotel management.
