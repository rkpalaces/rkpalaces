Welcome to RK Palace

Your Comfortable Stay in Puri

Welcome to Hotel RK Palace, your premier destination for a truly soulful and rejuvenating stay in the holy city of Puri. Perfectly nestled in the serene Baliapanda area, our hotel offers a unique blend of modern comfort and traditional Odia hospitality, just a few minutes' walk from the golden sands of Marine Drive Beach. At RK Palace, we believe that luxury should be accessible, which is why we provide meticulously designed, air-conditioned rooms that serve as a tranquil sanctuary after a day of exploring the city's spiritual and natural wonders.

Our strategic location ensures that while you enjoy the peace and quiet of a residential neighborhood, you remain within easy reach of the magnificent Lord Jagannath Temple, the bustling local markets, and the historic Puri Lighthouse. Whether you are a pilgrim seeking divine blessings, a family looking for a memorable beach vacation, or a solo traveler exploring the cultural richness of Odisha, our dedicated team is committed to making your stay exceptional.
